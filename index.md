## 📌 What is semaphore?

Semaphore serves as a visual indicator for your artificial intelligence coding assistant. When you work with tools like Cursor, Claude, or Copilot, you often wait for the agent to finish its work. Semaphore sits on your screen and shows you exactly what the agent does. You see at a glance if your agent stays idle, spends time thinking, or actively writes code in your editor. This tool saves you time by letting you know when to switch your focus back to the code.

## 🌟 Key features

*   **Real-time status tracking:** Follow the agent logic without checking the terminal or chat window.
*   **Minimal screen footprint:** The small interface stays out of your way while you work.
*   **Color-coded signals:** Bright colors show the states of your agent clearly.
*   **Window management:** Drag the light anywhere on your monitor.
*   **Low resource usage:** The app runs efficiently in the background without slowing your computer.

## 🛠️ How it works

Once you open the software, a small window appears. This window connects to your coding environment. It monitors the activity of your AI plugin. 

* **Idle (Grey):** The agent waits for your next prompt.
* **Thinking (Yellow):** The agent processes logic or examines your files.
* **Writing (Green):** The agent commits changes to your workspace.

You do not need to configure complex settings. The software detects your coding editor automatically. If you run multiple editors, the app highlights the one currently in focus.

## 🔧 Frequently asked questions

**Does this software store my code?**
No. Semaphore only watches the activity status of the agent. It never reads or uploads your private source code.

**What if the light stays grey?**
Close your coding editor and restart the semaphore app. Check that your AI plugin shows as active in your editor settings.

**Can I run multiple instances?**
The app handles multiple instances automatically. If you use two monitors, you can track different agents on each screen.

**Is this app demanding on my battery?**
The app consumes minimal power. It monitors signals rather than performing heavy computing tasks.
